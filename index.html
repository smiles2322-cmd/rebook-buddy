  3. Ask for these

    • A seat on the next flight, including on a partner airline.
    • Written confirmation (email or text) of anything they promise.

    Know your rights (United States)

    Under US Department of Transportation rules, if the airline cancels your flight for any reason and you choose not to travel, you can get a refund of your ticket, even if it was non-refundable. Accepting a new flight or a travel credit usually means giving up that refund, so decide before you say yes.

    Meals and hotels are not required by federal law. Many airlines promise them when the cancellation was their fault (like crew or maintenance), but not usually for weather. Rules and promises vary by airline and country, so check your airline's customer service plan.

    US DOT: Refunds page (opens in new tab)

    This is general information, not legal advice.
